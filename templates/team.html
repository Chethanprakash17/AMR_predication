Acknowledgments

We would like to acknowledge the following organizations and resources that made this project possible:

MIMIC-IV Database

This project uses data from the MIMIC-IV (Medical Information Mart for Intensive Care IV) database, a freely accessible critical care database provided by:

  • MIT Lab for Computational Physiology
  • PhysioNet - Platform for disseminating biomedical research data
  • Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center - Data source institution

We are grateful for the open-access philosophy that enables academic research and education in healthcare AI.

Faculty Guidance

We extend our gratitude to our faculty advisors and the Experiential Learning program at RV University for guidance, review, and support throughout this project.
